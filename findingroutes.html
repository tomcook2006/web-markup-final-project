Finding routes

Finding your way

When you're starting out it is best to look for flat routes in your area. A way to start is to go to a park/forest nearby and walk around to try and find 1 to 3 km routes that you can do as a loop. This is handy as if you feel you can't run any further then it's only a short distance back to where you started. Once you know your limits you can start challenging yourself by making bigger loops to run, you can use map apps like Google Maps to help find your own routes.

Altitude

Once you have been for a few runs on flat routes you can start experimenting with adding inclination. Look for some hillier routes in your area and follow the same steps as in finding your way. Adding inclination into your running helps further build leg strength and endurance. Also the good thing about going up a hill is the way down is a lot easier.
