About me

Hi I'm Tom. I started running at 9 years old doing park runs with my dad. When I started I was running a 28 minute 5k and a 1 hour 10k. After a year of running I decided to join a running club where we did lots of training to help our endurance; this brought my 5k time down to 24:30. This has inspired me to build this site to help beginner runners work towards their running goals.
