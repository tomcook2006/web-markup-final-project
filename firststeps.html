First steps

Starting running is simple! You don't actually need that many things to get started; most of the things you should already have at home.

Equipment

One of the most important things is a suitable pair of shoes. You don't necessarily need "running shoes" but you do want to make sure that the shoes you choose:

  • fit well (enough room for your toes to move)
  • provide cushioning to absorb the impact of the ground
  • are light
  • are breathable
  • are durable

Routes

When you start running, you won't know many routes. You may know a few but how do you know if they are beginner friendly? Use the routes page to help you find your first running route.

Nutrition

The right nutrition can make or break your run. Make sure to not eat a fatty meal at least two hours before your run so that your body can focus on the running instead of the digesting. Also try to have some quick carbs thirty minutes to an hour before your run for a quick energy boost.
